Government Loan Schemes

PMEGP

  • Purpose: Financial assistance for setting up new micro-enterprises.
  • Loan Amount: Up to project cost approved by bank.
  • Subsidy: Available based on category and location.
  • Best For: Manufacturing and service businesses.

PM Mudra Yojana

Categories:

Shishu Kishore Tarun Tarun Plus
  • Loan Amount: Up to ₹20 lakh.
  • Best For: Small businesses and startups.

Stand-Up India

  • Focus: For Women and SC/ST entrepreneurs.
  • Loan Amount: ₹10 lakh – ₹1 crore.
  • Best For: New businesses (Greenfield enterprises).

CGTMSE

  • Focus: Collateral-free business loans.
  • Support: Credit guarantee support for MSMEs.
  • Best For: Existing and new MSMEs requiring larger funds without collateral.

PM SVANidhi

  • Purpose: Working capital loans for street vendors.
  • Benefits: Digital transaction incentives and interest subsidy.
  • Best For: Street vendors and hawkers.

PM Vishwakarma

  • Focus: Support for artisans and craftsmen.
  • Benefits: Training, toolkit incentives, and credit support.
  • Best For: Traditional trades and crafts.
